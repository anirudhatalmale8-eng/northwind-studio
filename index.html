About

Northwind Studio is a small practice working in light, landscape and quiet detail.

We make photographs and printed work for people who would rather have one considered image than a dozen forgettable ones. Every commission starts with a conversation and a walk — usually in that order — and ends with files and prints we are happy to put our name to.

The studio works across editorial, architectural and personal commissions, and takes on a deliberately small number of projects each year. If that sounds like the right fit for what you have in mind, we would like to hear from you.
